AP Shipping Rules Tester for WooCommerce is in the [WordPress.org plugin directory](https://wordpress.org/plugins/ap-shipping-rules-tester-for-woocommerce/). Version 1.2.8 is the release you install.

Open **WooCommerce > Shipping Rules Tester** after you activate it. The screen reads your shipping settings. It doesn't change them, and it doesn't save the sample you type in.

Give it a destination and a package. It shows the zone that matches, then the local rates WooCommerce calculates for built-in flat rate, free shipping, and local pickup. You can use a quick package, or build several items, including a saved product's shipping class, weight, and dimensions.

Some methods stay marked as skipped. The tester doesn't build a live cart, apply a coupon, sign in as a customer, or call an external shipping provider. Installed extensions can still run their own hooks during the calculation, so confirm a surprising result at checkout.

The side-by-side comparison stays in that browser tab. Leave the page, or choose Clear comparison, and it's gone.
